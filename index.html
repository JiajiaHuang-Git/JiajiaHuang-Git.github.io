About Me

I am a PhD student in computational physics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and a member of the Ångström Group, led by Prof. Ding Pan.

I received my B.S. degree from Beijing Normal University. My research focuses on molecular systems, connecting first-principles simulations with machine learning to understand molecular behavior and spectroscopy.

For research code and projects, visit Jiajia Huang’s GitHub profile.

Research Interests

My research interests include AI for Science, machine-learning molecular dynamics, and first-principles spectroscopy. I am particularly interested in molecular motion, dipole moments, and infrared spectra under extreme pressure and temperature conditions.
