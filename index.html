About

I am a PhD student in computational physics at HKUST and a member of the Ångström Group, led by Prof. Ding Pan.

My research interests lie at the intersection of first-principles simulations, molecular dynamics and machine learning. I am interested in using these tools to understand molecular behavior and spectroscopy, including systems under extreme pressure and temperature.
